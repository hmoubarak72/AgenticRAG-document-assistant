# Northwind Outfitters — Customer Policy (Sample Document)

*This is a fictional sample document included so you can try the assistant immediately.*

## Section 1 — Scope

This policy applies to all purchases made through the Northwind Outfitters online store
and its retail locations in the United States.

## Section 2 — Orders

2.1 Orders are confirmed by email once payment has been authorized.
2.2 Customers may cancel an order free of charge until it has been shipped.

## Section 3 — Shipping

3.1 Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days.
3.2 Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and is charged at checkout.
3.3 International orders ship within 10 business days; customs duties are paid by the customer.

## Section 4 — Returns and Refunds

4.1 Unused items in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery.

4.2 Refunds. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within
7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Original shipping
fees are non-refundable unless the item arrived damaged or the wrong item was sent.
Refunds for international orders exclude customs duties and taxes already paid.

4.3 Items marked "Final Sale" cannot be returned or refunded.

4.4 Store credit may be offered instead of a refund for returns made between 31 and
60 days after delivery.

## Section 5 — Warranty

5.1 Outdoor equipment carries a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects.
5.2 Warranty claims require proof of purchase and photos of the defect.

## Section 6 — Data Retention

6.1 Order records are retained for seven years to meet accounting obligations.
6.2 The Customer Support department is responsible for deleting marketing data on request
within 30 days.
